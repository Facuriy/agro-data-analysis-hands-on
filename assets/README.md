These are real, downsampled RGB field previews with no EXIF metadata. They are suitable for visual orientation, plot overlays, and teaching-scale crop inspection.

They are independently display-stretched and are **not radiometric products**. Do not calculate calibrated cross-date vegetation change from them. Full-resolution rasters, georeferencing, CRS information, and source filenames are not released here.
